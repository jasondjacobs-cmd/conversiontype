How to convert ounces to grams

1 avoirdupois ounce = 28.349523125 grams exactly. Convert food portions, recipes, packages, and small-object weights into grams.

Formula

grams = ounces × 28.349523125. Apply this conversion factor to the ounces value to get grams.

Examples

  • 1 oz = 28.349523125 g
  • 8 oz = 226.796185 g
  • 16 oz = 453.59237 g
