Percentage change formula

Subtract the old value from the new value, divide by the absolute old value, then multiply by 100. A positive result is an increase and a negative result is a decrease.

Examples

  • 80 to 100 = 25%
  • 100 to 80 = -20%
  • 50 to 75 = 50%
  • 200 to 250 = 25%
