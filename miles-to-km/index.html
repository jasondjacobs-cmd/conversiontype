How to convert miles to kilometers

1 mile = 1.609344 kilometers exactly. Convert driving distances, running routes, and map measurements into metric units.

Formula

kilometers = miles × 1.609344. Apply this conversion factor to the miles value to get kilometers.

Examples

  • 5 mi = 8.04672 km
  • 10 mi = 16.09344 km
  • 26.2 mi = 42.1648128 km
