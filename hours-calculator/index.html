How this hours calculator works

Subtract the start time from the end time. When the end is earlier, the calculator treats it as the next day.

Examples

  • 8:15 AM to 4:45 PM is 8 hours 30 minutes
  • 10:00 PM to 2:00 AM is 4 hours
  • 9:30 AM to 9:30 AM is 0 hours
