Formula and method

Deck area = length × width. Board coverage uses board face width plus gap and board length, with waste added.

Worked example

A 16 × 12 ft deck is 192 ft² before adding waste and converting coverage to whole boards.

Planning notes

Material sizes, coverage, waste and installation requirements vary by product and project. Use this estimate for planning and verify quantities against product specifications and local project requirements before purchasing.
