How to convert inches to centimeters

1 inch = 2.54 centimeters exactly. Translate screen sizes, lumber dimensions, sewing measurements, and product specifications into metric units.

Formula

centimeters = inches × 2.54. Apply this conversion factor to the inches value to get centimeters.

Examples

  • 2 in = 5.08 cm
  • 12 in = 30.48 cm
  • 40 in = 101.6 cm
