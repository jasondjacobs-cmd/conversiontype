Markup vs. margin

Markup divides profit by cost. Margin divides profit by selling price. If an item costs $60 and sells for $100, markup is 66.666667% and margin is 40%.

Examples

  • $60 cost, $100 price = 66.666667% markup
  • $60 cost, $100 price = 40% margin
  • $80 cost, $100 price = 25% markup
  • $80 cost, $100 price = 20% margin
