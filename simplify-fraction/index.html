How to simplify a fraction

Find the greatest common divisor of the numerator and denominator, then divide both by that number.

Formula

Find the greatest common divisor of the numerator and denominator, then divide both by that number.

Examples

  • 18/24 = 3/4
  • 1/2 = 0.5
  • 1/4 = 25%
  • 50% = 1/2
