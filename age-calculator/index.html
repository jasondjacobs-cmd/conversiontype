How this age calculator works

Subtract complete calendar years, then complete months, then remaining days. February 29 birthdays use the last valid day in non-leap years.

Examples

  • A target date before the birth date is rejected
  • Leap-day births remain valid
  • Total days use calendar dates rather than elapsed milliseconds
