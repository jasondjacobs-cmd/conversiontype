Affordability method

Gross income is income before deductions. Debt-to-income ratio (DTI) compares monthly obligations with gross monthly income. This tool uses editable 28% housing and 36% total DTI planning assumptions, then applies the tighter limit.

Worked example

$120,000 annual gross income equals $10,000 monthly. A 28% housing ratio gives $2,800; a 36% total ratio less $500 monthly debt gives $3,100, so the housing ratio limits the estimate.

Important limitation

This is a budgeting estimate, not mortgage preapproval, underwriting, or a lending decision.
