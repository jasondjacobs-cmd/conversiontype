Rent vs buy method

The model tracks mortgage amortization, home appreciation, equity, maintenance costs, selling costs, rent growth, and opportunity cost from investing available cash and monthly savings. Investment return and appreciation are assumptions, not forecasts.

Worked example

Changing the ownership horizon can materially change the result because buying and selling transaction costs occur around the purchase and sale while equity builds over time.

Interpretation

The result is assumption-sensitive. It does not model income-tax deductions, capital-gains taxes, or lender qualification. Compare the underlying values rather than treating the output as financial advice.
