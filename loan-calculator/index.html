How this calculator works

For a fixed-rate loan, M = P × r(1+r)ⁿ ÷ ((1+r)ⁿ − 1). The monthly rate is the annual interest rate divided by 12. At 0% interest, payment equals principal divided by the number of months.

Worked example

A $25,000 loan at 7.5% for 5 years has a monthly payment of about $500.95.

Assumptions and interpretation

This estimate assumes equal monthly payments, a fixed rate, and no fees. It is not a lender disclosure.
