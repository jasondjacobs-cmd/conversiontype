Butter measurement equivalents

One US stick of butter is 4 weight ounces, 8 tablespoons, one-half US cup, or about 113.398 grams. One US cup is two sticks.

Formula

The converter changes the source amount to grams, then converts that mass to the selected butter unit.

Examples

  • 1 stick ≈ 113.398 g
  • 2 sticks = 1 US cup
  • 1 US cup = 16 tablespoons
  • 1 stick = 4 oz by weight
