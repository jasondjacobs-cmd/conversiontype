How to use the Ratio Calculator

Choose the ratio calculation that matches your problem, enter the known values, and select Calculate. The result shows the answer and the calculation used.

Formula

A ratio compares two quantities in the form a:b. Equivalent ratios multiply or divide both values by the same nonzero number.

Examples

  • Simplify 24:36 to 2:3
  • Solve 2:3 = 4:x to get x = 6
  • Scale 3:2 by 4 to get 12:8
  • Convert 3:4 to 75%
