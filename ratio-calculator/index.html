How to use the ratio calculator

Choose the calculation you need, enter positive numbers, and select the calculation button. The result appears immediately with a short explanation.

What is a ratio?

A ratio compares two quantities in a set order. The ratio 3:2 means there are three units of the first quantity for every two units of the second quantity.

How do you simplify a ratio?

Divide both sides by their greatest common divisor. For 150:100, the greatest common divisor is 50, so the simplified ratio is 3:2.

How do you solve an equivalent ratio?

Use cross multiplication. In 3:4 = 9:x, multiply 4 by 9 and divide by 3. The missing value is 12.

Frequently asked questions

Can a ratio contain decimals?

Yes. The simplifier converts finite decimals to whole numbers before reducing the ratio.

Does the calculator upload my values?

No. Every calculation runs locally in your browser.
