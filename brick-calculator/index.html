Estimate bricks for a wall using wall area, openings, brick size, mortar joint and waste.

Formula and method

Bricks = net wall area ÷ nominal brick face area, with mortar joint and waste included.

Worked example

A wall's gross area is reduced by openings before dividing by the nominal brick-and-joint face area.

Planning notes

Material sizes, coverage, waste and installation requirements vary by product and project. Use this estimate for planning and verify quantities against product specifications and local project requirements before purchasing.
