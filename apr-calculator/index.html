How this calculator works

The calculator solves net proceeds = payment × (1 − (1 + i)⁻ⁿ) ÷ i for the monthly rate i, then reports nominal annual APR as 12i.

Worked example

If a $10,000 loan deducts $500 in included charges, the cash received is $9,500 even though payments may be based on $10,000.

Assumptions and interpretation

Estimated APR uses evenly timed monthly payments and included upfront charges. It does not create an official Truth in Lending disclosure.
