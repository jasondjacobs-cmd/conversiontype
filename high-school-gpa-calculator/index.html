Formula

Unweighted GPA = Σ(grade points × credits) ÷ Σ(credits). Weighted GPA adds the selected course-level boost to each grade-point value before weighting by credits. The default convention is Regular +0.0, Honors +0.5, and AP/IB +1.0.

Worked examples

  • Equal-credit A Regular, B Honors, and A− AP courses produce a 3.57 unweighted GPA and 4.07 weighted GPA with the defaults.
  • An A in an AP/IB course is 5.0 weighted points under the default +1.0 convention; weighted GPA is not capped at 4.0.

Weighted GPA policies vary

Weighted GPA gives extra grade points for advanced courses. Schools may use different Honors, AP, and IB boosts or entirely different scales, so this result is an estimate unless the defaults match your school's policy.
