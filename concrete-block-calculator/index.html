Concrete Block Calculator

Estimate concrete masonry blocks for a wall with openings and waste.

Formula and method

Blocks = net wall area ÷ nominal block face area, then adjusted for waste and rounded up.

Worked example

An 8 × 16 inch nominal block covers about 0.889 ft² before waste.

Planning notes

Material sizes, coverage, waste and installation requirements vary by product and project. Use this estimate for planning and verify quantities against product specifications and local project requirements before purchasing.
