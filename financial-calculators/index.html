How to use financial calculators

Financial calculators turn principal, interest rate, loan term, payments, or finance charges into estimates you can compare. Check the stated assumptions and use the method that matches your loan or interest agreement.

These free tools provide estimates and educational calculations. They are not lender disclosures, offers, financial advice, or guarantees.
