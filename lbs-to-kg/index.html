How to convert pounds to kilograms

1 pound = 0.45359237 kilograms exactly. Translate package weights, gym plates, and product specifications into metric mass.

Formula

kilograms = pounds × 0.45359237. Apply this conversion factor to the pounds value to get kilograms.

Examples

  • 10 lb = 4.5359237 kg
  • 100 lb = 45.359237 kg
  • 200 lb = 90.718474 kg
