How this days from today calculator works

Add or subtract whole calendar days from the selected local calendar date, then report the resulting date and weekday.

Examples

  • 7 days from a date falls on the same weekday next week
  • 30 days may cross into the next month
  • 365 days may cross a leap day

Common day offsets

Use 7, 14, 28, 30, 45, 60, 90, 100, 180, or 365 days for common planning intervals. The calculator uses your selected base date rather than a hard-coded date.
