Why cups to grams depends on the ingredient

Cups measure volume and grams measure mass. Flour, sugar, honey, oats and other ingredients have different densities.

Formula

grams = cups × grams per cup for the selected ingredient. Recipe references can differ slightly because packing and measuring methods vary.

Examples

  • 1 cup all-purpose flour ≈ 120 g
  • 1 cup granulated sugar ≈ 200 g
  • 1 cup butter ≈ 226.796185 g
  • 1 cup rolled oats ≈ 90 g
