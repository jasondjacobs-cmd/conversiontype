How to find what percent one number is of another

Divide the part by the total and multiply by 100. For 45 out of 150, 45 ÷ 150 × 100 = 30%.

Examples

  • 45 of 150 = 30%
  • 12 of 80 = 15%
  • 3 of 8 = 37.5%
  • 75 of 100 = 75%
