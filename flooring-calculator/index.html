Formula and method

Order area = length × width × (1 + waste rate). Boxes = order area ÷ coverage per box, rounded up.

Worked example

A 15 × 12 ft room with 10% waste requires 198 ft² of coverage before rounding to boxes.

Planning notes

Material sizes, coverage, waste and installation requirements vary by product and project. Use this estimate for planning and verify quantities against product specifications and local project requirements before purchasing.
