How to calculate percentages

Choose the calculation that matches your question. Enter the values and select Calculate. Each result includes the formula used.

Examples

  • 20% of 150 = 30
  • 45 is 30% of 150
  • 80 to 100 = 25% increase
  • 25% off $80 = $60
