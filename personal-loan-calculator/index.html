How this calculator works

Monthly payment uses the fixed-rate amortization formula. Origination fee = loan amount × fee percentage; net proceeds = loan amount − fee.

Worked example

A 3% fee on a $15,000 loan is $450, so estimated net proceeds are $14,550.

Assumptions and interpretation

The origination fee is reported separately from the nominal interest rate. It is not simply added to the interest rate.
