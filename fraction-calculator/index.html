How to calculate fractions

Use a common denominator for addition or subtraction. Multiply numerators and denominators for multiplication. Multiply by the reciprocal for division.

Formula

Use a common denominator for addition or subtraction. Multiply numerators and denominators for multiplication. Multiply by the reciprocal for division.

Examples

  • 1/2 + 1/3 = 5/6
  • 1/2 = 0.5
  • 1/4 = 25%
  • 50% = 1/2
