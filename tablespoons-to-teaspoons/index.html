How to convert tablespoons to teaspoons

One US tablespoon equals 3 US teaspoons. Australian tablespoons use a different volume, so this calculator should not be used as an Australian tablespoon conversion.

Formula

teaspoons = tablespoons × 3.

Examples

  • 1 tbsp = 3 tsp
  • 2 tbsp = 6 tsp
  • 4 tbsp = 12 tsp
