Closing cost method

Closing costs include loan costs and other costs such as government fees, prepaid expenses, and escrow deposits, minus lender or seller credits. Cash to close adds the down payment and subtracts deposits already paid.

Worked example

An $80,000 down payment, $12,000 gross costs, $2,000 credits, and $5,000 deposit produces $10,000 net closing costs and $85,000 estimated cash to close.

Assumptions

Fee defaults are zero because actual charges vary. Use a Loan Estimate and Closing Disclosure for transaction-specific figures.
