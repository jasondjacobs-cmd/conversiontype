How this work hours calculator works

Find elapsed shift time, account for crossing midnight, then subtract the break duration.

Examples

  • 8:00 AM to 4:30 PM with a 30-minute break is 8 hours
  • 7:00 AM to 3:00 PM with no break is 8 hours
  • 10:00 PM to 6:00 AM with a 30-minute break is 7 hours 30 minutes
