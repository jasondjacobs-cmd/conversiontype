Payoff method

Each month, interest is calculated from the remaining principal balance. Extra principal reduces the balance after the scheduled payment, shortening the payoff date without modeling a lender recast.

Worked example

At 0% interest, a $120,000 balance over 120 months is $1,000 per month. Adding $1,000 monthly pays it off in 60 months with no interest.

Assumptions

Annual extra principal is applied every twelfth month. A one-time extra payment is applied in the selected payment month.
