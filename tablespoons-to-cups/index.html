How to convert tablespoons to cups

One US cup contains 16 US tablespoons.

Formula

cups = tablespoons ÷ 16.

Examples

  • 4 tbsp = 0.25 cup
  • 8 tbsp = 0.5 cup
  • 16 tbsp = 1 cup
