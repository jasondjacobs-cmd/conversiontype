Final Grade Calculator

Find what you need on your final exam or project your course grade from an expected final score.

Calculate your final grade

Formula

Required final score = [target grade − current grade × (1 − final weight)] ÷ final weight. Projected course grade = current grade × (1 − final weight) + final score × final weight, with weights written as decimals.

Worked examples

  • With an 85% current grade, a 90% target, and a final worth 30%, you need 101.67% on the final. A score above 100% may require extra credit.
  • With an 85% current grade and a 92% final worth 30%, the projected course grade is 87.10%.

What does final exam weight mean?

Final exam weight is the share of the overall course grade assigned to the final. A 30% final means the rest of the course contributes 70%. Check your syllabus because grading policies vary.
