How this calculator works

A = P(1 + r/n)ⁿᵗ. End-of-period recurring contributions use C × ((1 + r/n)ⁿᵗ − 1) ÷ (r/n).

Worked example

At monthly compounding, a $100 contribution is added at the end of each month.

Assumptions and interpretation

Rates stay fixed and contributions occur at the end of each compounding period. This is a mathematical projection, not investment advice.
