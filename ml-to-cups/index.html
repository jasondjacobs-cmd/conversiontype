How to convert milliliters to cups

Divide milliliters by 236.5882365 for US customary cups or by 250 for metric cups.

Formula

cups = milliliters ÷ milliliters per selected cup.

Examples

  • 236.5882365 mL = 1 US cup
  • 473.176473 mL = 2 US cups
  • 250 mL = 1 metric cup
