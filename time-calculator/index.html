How this time calculator works

Convert both durations to seconds, add or subtract them, then normalize the result. Clock calculations wrap across midnight and report the day change.

Examples

  • 1 hour plus 45 minutes equals 1 hour 45 minutes
  • 0 hours plus 90 minutes normalizes to 1 hour 30 minutes
  • 11:30 PM plus 2 hours is 1:30 AM the next day
