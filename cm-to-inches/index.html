How to convert centimeters to inches

1 inch = 2.54 centimeters exactly. Measure furniture, screens, crafts, and body dimensions across metric and US customary specifications.

Formula

inches = centimeters ÷ 2.54. Apply this conversion factor to the centimeters value to get inches.

Examples

  • 2 cm = 0.787401574803 in
  • 30 cm = 11.811023622 in
  • 100 cm = 39.3700787402 in
