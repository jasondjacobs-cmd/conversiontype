How to convert kilograms to pounds

1 pound = 0.45359237 kilograms exactly. Compare package weights, gym plates, ingredients, and product specifications.

Formula

pounds = kilograms ÷ 0.45359237. Apply this conversion factor to the kilograms value to get pounds.

Examples

  • 1 kg = 2.20462262185 lb
  • 50 kg = 110.231131093 lb
