How to convert meters to feet

1 foot = 0.3048 meters exactly. Express metric lengths, room sizes, track distances, and heights in feet.

Formula

feet = meters ÷ 0.3048. Apply this conversion factor to the meters value to get feet.

Examples

  • 1 m = 3.28083989501 ft
  • 10 m = 32.8083989501 ft
  • 100 m = 328.083989501 ft
