Mortgage payment formula

For a fixed-rate mortgage, M = P × r(1+r)ⁿ ÷ ((1+r)ⁿ − 1). Principal is the amount borrowed, interest is the cost of borrowing, and loan-to-value (LTV) compares the loan with the home's price. At 0% interest, payment is principal divided by months.

Worked example

A $400,000 home with $80,000 down creates a $320,000 mortgage. At 6% for 30 years, principal and interest are about $1,918.56 monthly. Adding $6,000 annual property tax and $1,800 insurance produces about $2,568.56 before HOA or mortgage insurance.

Assumptions

This is a planning estimate, not a lender disclosure. PMI, taxes, insurance, and HOA costs vary.
