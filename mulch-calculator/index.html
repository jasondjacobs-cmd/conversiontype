Estimate cubic yards and bags of mulch for landscape beds.

Formula

Cubic feet = area in square feet × depth in inches ÷ 12. Cubic yards = cubic feet ÷ 27. Bag purchase quantity rounds up to a whole bag.

Worked example

A 20 × 10 ft bed at 3 inches deep requires 50 ft³, or about 1.852 yd³.
