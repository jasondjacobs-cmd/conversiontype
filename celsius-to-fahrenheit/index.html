How to convert celsius to fahrenheit

The Celsius and Fahrenheit scales use different zero points and degree sizes. Compare weather reports and cooking temperatures. Water freezes at 0°C and boils at 100°C at standard atmospheric pressure.

Formula

°F = (°C × 9 ÷ 5) + 32. Apply this conversion factor to the celsius value to get fahrenheit.

Examples

  • 0 °C = 32 °F
  • 20 °C = 68 °F
  • 100 °C = 212 °F

Weather and oven references

Common oven settings include 180°C = 356°F and 200°C = 392°F.
