How this calculator works

Amount financed = vehicle price − down payment − trade-in credit + applicable tax + fees. Payment then uses the fixed-rate amortization formula.

Worked example

On a $35,000 vehicle with $5,000 down and $3,000 trade-in, tax and fees are added before the loan payment is calculated.

Assumptions and interpretation

Tax is estimated on vehicle price minus trade-in credit. Rules differ by location. Results are estimates, not lender disclosures.
