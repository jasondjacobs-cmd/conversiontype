Fence Calculator

Estimate fence sections, posts, rails and pickets from fence length and spacing.

Formula and method

Sections = fence length ÷ post spacing, rounded up. Posts = sections + 1. Rails and pickets follow the selected spacing.

Worked example

A 100 ft fence with 8 ft post spacing requires 13 sections and 14 line/end posts before gates or special corners.

Planning notes

Material sizes, coverage, waste and installation requirements vary by product and project. Use this estimate for planning and verify quantities against product specifications and local project requirements before purchasing.
