Why the ingredient matters

There is no universal grams-to-cups factor. Ingredients have different densities, so the same cup volume can weigh very different amounts.

Formula

cups = grams ÷ grams per cup for the selected ingredient. Recipe references can use slightly different ingredient weights because measuring and packing methods vary.

Examples

  • 120 g all-purpose flour ≈ 1 cup
  • 200 g granulated sugar ≈ 1 cup
  • 226.796185 g butter ≈ 1 cup
  • 340 g honey ≈ 1 cup
