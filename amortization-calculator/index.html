How this calculator works

The regular payment uses M = P × r(1+r)ⁿ ÷ ((1+r)ⁿ − 1). Each row applies interest to the prior balance and sends the remainder to principal.

Worked example

Early payments on a long fixed-rate loan usually contain more interest; later payments contain more principal.

Assumptions and interpretation

The schedule uses full internal precision and adjusts the final payment so the remaining balance reaches zero.
