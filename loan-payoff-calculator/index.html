How this calculator works

Each month applies interest first, then payment, principal reduction, and the new balance. The final payment is adjusted to the exact amount due.

Worked example

Adding $100 to a $400 regular payment reduces principal faster and can shorten payoff time.

Assumptions and interpretation

The rate and payments are assumed fixed, with no new fees or charges. Payments that do not reduce principal are rejected.
