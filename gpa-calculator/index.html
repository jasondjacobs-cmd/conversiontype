Formula

GPA = Σ(grade points × credit hours) ÷ Σ(credit hours). Each course's grade points multiplied by credits are its quality points.

Default grading scale

The default scale is A 4.0, A− 3.7, B+ 3.3, B 3.0, B− 2.7, C+ 2.3, C 2.0, C− 1.7, D+ 1.3, D 1.0, D− 0.7, F 0.0. Schools use different grading scales and GPA policies; edit the scale to match your institution.

Worked examples

  • A for 3 credits, B+ for 4, and B− for 3 gives 33.3 quality points over 10 credits, or a 3.33 GPA.
  • A for 1 credit and C for 4 credits gives 12 quality points over 5 credits, or 2.40—not the simple average of 4.0 and 2.0.

Pass/fail, withdrawals, repeats, incompletes and transfer-credit treatment vary by institution and are not modeled automatically.
