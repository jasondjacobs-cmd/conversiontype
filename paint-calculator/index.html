Formula

Paintable area = gross wall area − excluded openings. Coverage required = paintable area × coats. Gallons = coverage required ÷ coverage per gallon.

Coverage assumption

Coverage varies by paint, surface and application. The default is only a starting estimate, so enter the coverage stated for the paint you plan to use.
