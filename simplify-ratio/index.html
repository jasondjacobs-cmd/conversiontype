How to simplify a ratio

Find the greatest common divisor of both numbers, then divide each side by that value. For 24:36, the greatest common divisor is 12, so 24:36 simplifies to 2:3.

Examples

  • 24:36 = 2:3
  • 10:15 = 2:3
  • 16:9 = 16:9
  • 100:25 = 4:1
