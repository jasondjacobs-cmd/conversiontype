Refinance method

Refinancing replaces the remaining balance with a new loan. The break-even point divides cash closing costs by positive monthly payment savings. Financing costs increases the new principal rather than making those costs disappear.

Worked example

If a refinance lowers monthly principal and interest by $200 and costs $6,000 in cash, the simple payment break-even is 30 months.

Interpretation

A lower payment can still increase lifetime interest when the new term is longer. Compare payment, remaining interest, new interest, and payoff timing together.
