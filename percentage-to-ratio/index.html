How to convert a percentage to a ratio

Write the percentage as a value compared with 100, remove decimals if needed, then reduce both sides by their greatest common divisor.

Formula

p% becomes p:100. For 75%, 75:100 reduces by 25 to 3:4.

Examples

  • 75% = 3:4
  • 25% = 1:4
  • 50% = 1:2
  • 12.5% = 1:8
