How this business days calculator works

Business days are Monday through Friday. Saturdays, Sundays, and the default starting date are excluded. Public holidays are not removed.

Examples

  • Friday + 1 business day is Monday
  • Monday to the next Monday is 5 business days by default
  • Subtracting 5 business days moves back one workweek
