How to convert kilometers to miles

1 mile = 1.609344 kilometers exactly. Compare road distances, race lengths, travel plans, and map measurements.

Formula

miles = kilometers ÷ 1.609344. Apply this conversion factor to the kilometers value to get miles.

Examples

  • 5 km = 3.10685596119 mi
  • 10 km = 6.21371192237 mi
  • 42.195 km = 26.2187574565 mi
