How to convert cups to tablespoons

One US cup contains 16 US tablespoons.

Formula

tablespoons = cups × 16.

Examples

  • 1/4 cup = 4 tbsp
  • 1/2 cup = 8 tbsp
  • 1 cup = 16 tbsp
