How to scale a ratio

Multiply both values in the ratio by the same scale factor. A 3:2 ratio scaled by 4 becomes 12:8.

Examples

  • 3:2 × 4 = 12:8
  • 16:9 × 2 = 32:18
  • 5:1 × 0.5 = 2.5:0.5
  • 4:3 × 3 = 12:9
