How to scale a ratio

Multiply each side of the original ratio by one factor. The new ratio stays equivalent to the original ratio.

Formula

Scaled ratio = (a × factor):(b × factor). For 3:2 scaled by 4, the answer is 12:8.

Examples

  • 3:2 × 4 = 12:8
  • 16:9 × 2 = 32:18
  • 5:4 × 3 = 15:12
  • 1:3 × 10 = 10:30
