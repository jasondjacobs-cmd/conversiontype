Formula

New cumulative GPA = (existing GPA × existing credits + new quality points) ÷ total credits. When combining semesters, cumulative GPA = Σ(semester GPA × semester credits) ÷ Σ(semester credits).

Worked examples

  • A 3.20 GPA over 60 credits plus 15 credits at a 3.60 semester GPA gives a new cumulative GPA of 3.28.
  • A 3.50 semester over 12 credits and a 3.00 semester over 18 credits combine to 3.20, not the simple average 3.25.

Why credits matter

Cumulative GPA is credit weighted. A semester with more completed credits contributes more to the overall GPA than a semester with fewer credits.
