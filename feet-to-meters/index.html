How to convert feet to meters

1 foot = 0.3048 meters exactly. Convert room dimensions, building heights, elevation, and sports-field measurements.

Formula

meters = feet × 0.3048. Apply this conversion factor to the feet value to get meters.

Examples

  • 3 ft = 0.9144 m
  • 6 ft = 1.8288 m
  • 100 ft = 30.48 m
