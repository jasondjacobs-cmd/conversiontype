Formula

In points mode, grade = total points earned ÷ total points possible × 100. In weighted mode, current grade = Σ(score × weight) ÷ Σ(weights). If only part of the course has been graded, the calculator normalizes the completed weight instead of treating remaining work as zero.

Worked examples

  • 85/100 + 42/50 + 92/100 = 219/250 = 87.60%.
  • 90% at 30%, 80% at 50%, and 95% at 20% gives 86.00%.

About weighted grades

A weighted grade gives assignments or categories different influence on the course result. If your syllabus uses category weights, enter each score and its category weight. School policies vary, so use the weights in your course syllabus.
