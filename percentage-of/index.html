How to find a percentage of a number

Divide the percentage by 100, then multiply by the number. For 20% of 150, calculate 0.20 × 150 to get 30.

Examples

  • 20% of 150 = 30
  • 15% of 80 = 12
  • 5% of 240 = 12
  • 75% of 60 = 45
