How to convert fluid ounces to cups

There are 8 US fluid ounces in 1 US cup. Fluid ounces measure volume.

Formula

cups = fluid ounces ÷ 8.

Examples

  • 2 fl oz = 0.25 cup
  • 8 fl oz = 1 cup
  • 16 fl oz = 2 cups
