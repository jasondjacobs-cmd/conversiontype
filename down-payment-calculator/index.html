Down payment formula

Down payment percentage = down payment ÷ home price × 100. Loan-to-value (LTV) = loan amount ÷ home price × 100. Mortgage insurance rules vary by loan program and lender, so this tool does not declare PMI required from LTV alone.

Worked example

$80,000 down on a $400,000 home is 20%, leaving a $320,000 loan and 80% LTV.
