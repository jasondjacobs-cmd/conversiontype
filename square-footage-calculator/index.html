Area Formula

Rectangle: A = length × width. Circle: A = πr². Triangle: A = base × height ÷ 2. Trapezoid: A = (a + b) × height ÷ 2.

Worked example

A 12 ft × 10 ft rectangle is 120 square feet, or 13.333 square yards.
