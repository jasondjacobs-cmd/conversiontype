How to convert fahrenheit to celsius

The Fahrenheit and Celsius scales use different zero points and degree sizes. Translate weather reports and cooking temperatures. Water freezes at 32°F and boils at 212°F at standard atmospheric pressure.

Formula

°C = (°F − 32) × 5 ÷ 9. Apply this conversion factor to the fahrenheit value to get celsius.

Examples

  • 32 °F = 0 °C
  • 68 °F = 20 °C
  • 212 °F = 100 °C

Weather and oven references

Common oven settings include 350°F ≈ 176.666666667°C and 400°F ≈ 204.444444444°C.
