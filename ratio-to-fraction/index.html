How to convert a ratio to a fraction

Write the first ratio value as the numerator and the second as the denominator. Then reduce the fraction by their greatest common divisor.

Examples

  • 3:4 = 3/4
  • 2:6 = 1/3
  • 5:10 = 1/2
  • 16:9 = 16/9
