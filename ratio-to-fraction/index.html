How to convert a ratio to a fraction

Place the first ratio value over the second value, then divide the numerator and denominator by their greatest common divisor.

Formula

a:b becomes a/b. For 2:6, write 2/6 and divide both values by 2 to get 1/3.

Examples

  • 2:6 = 1/3
  • 3:5 = 3/5
  • 8:12 = 2/3
  • 16:9 = 16/9
