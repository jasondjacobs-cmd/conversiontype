How to convert a ratio to a percentage

Divide the first ratio value by the second, then multiply by 100. The result expresses the first value as a percentage of the second.

Formula

Percentage = (a ÷ b) × 100. For 3:4, 3 ÷ 4 × 100 = 75%.

Examples

  • 3:4 = 75%
  • 1:2 = 50%
  • 2:5 = 40%
  • 7:8 = 87.5%
