How to convert a ratio to a percentage

Divide the first value by the second, then multiply by 100. For 3:4, 3 ÷ 4 × 100 = 75%.

Examples

  • 1:4 = 25%
  • 1:5 = 20%
  • 2:5 = 40%
  • 3:4 = 75%
