Amortization formula and method

Amortization divides a fixed-rate loan into scheduled payments. Each payment first covers interest on the principal balance; the remainder reduces principal. The final payment absorbs the remaining balance.

Worked example

A $12,000 loan at 0% for one year has twelve $1,000 principal payments and ends with a $0 balance.
