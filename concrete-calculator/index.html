Formula

Rectangular volume = length × width × depth. Circular volume = π × radius² × height. Cubic yards = cubic feet ÷ 27. Waste is added after base volume.

Bag estimates

This calculator uses approximate yields of 0.30 ft³ for a 40 lb bag, 0.45 ft³ for a 60 lb bag and 0.60 ft³ for an 80 lb bag. Check the product label before purchasing.
