How to convert cups to fluid ounces

A US customary cup contains 8 US fluid ounces. Fluid ounces measure volume, not weight.

Formula

fluid ounces = cups × 8.

Examples

  • 1 cup = 8 fl oz
  • 2 cups = 16 fl oz
  • 4 cups = 32 fl oz
