How this date calculator works

Days and weeks use calendar-day counting. Months and years preserve the day when possible and clamp to the final valid day otherwise.

Examples

  • January 31, 2028 + 1 month = February 29, 2028
  • February 29, 2028 + 1 year = February 28, 2029
  • December 31 + 1 day moves to January 1
