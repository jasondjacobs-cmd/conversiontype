How to convert grams to ounces

1 avoirdupois ounce = 28.349523125 grams exactly. Convert recipe quantities, food portions, and product weights into ounces.

Formula

ounces = grams ÷ 28.349523125. Apply this conversion factor to the grams value to get ounces.

Examples

  • 100 g = 3.52739619496 oz
  • 500 g = 17.6369809748 oz
  • 1000 g = 35.2739619496 oz
