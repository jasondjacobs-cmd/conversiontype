Method

Riser count is the smallest whole number that keeps each riser at or below the entered target. Actual riser height = total rise ÷ risers. Standard tread count is one fewer than riser count. Stringer length uses the Pythagorean theorem.

Important

This calculator provides geometry, not a code-compliance determination. Stair requirements vary by jurisdiction and project.
