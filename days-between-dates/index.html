How this days between dates calculator works

The default excludes the starting date. Inclusive counting adds one day in the direction of the interval.

Examples

  • March 1 to March 31 is 30 days by default
  • March 1 through March 31 is 31 days inclusively
  • A seven-day difference equals 1 week
