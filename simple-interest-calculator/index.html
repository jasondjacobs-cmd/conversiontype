How this calculator works

I = P × r × t and A = P + I. The annual percentage rate is written as a decimal in the calculation.

Worked example

$10,000 at 5% simple interest for 3 years earns $1,500 and ends at $11,500.

Assumptions and interpretation

Simple interest does not earn interest on prior interest.
