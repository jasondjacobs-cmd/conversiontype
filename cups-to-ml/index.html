How to convert cups to milliliters

A US customary cup is 236.5882365 mL. A metric cup is 250 mL. Select the standard used by your recipe.

Formula

milliliters = cups × milliliters per selected cup.

Examples

  • 1 US cup = 236.5882365 mL
  • 2 US cups = 473.176473 mL
  • 1 metric cup = 250 mL
