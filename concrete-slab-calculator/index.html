Formula

Slab volume = length × width × thickness × quantity. After converting to cubic feet, cubic yards = cubic feet ÷ 27. Waste is applied after base volume.

Worked example

A 20 ft × 12 ft slab that is 4 inches thick has a base volume of 80 ft³, or about 2.963 yd³ before waste.

Assumptions

Bag yields and waste are estimates. Project thickness and reinforcement requirements depend on the project and local requirements.
